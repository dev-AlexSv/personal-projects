Computer Systems Servicing

Gain practical skills in hardware, software installation, troubleshooting, and maintenance of computer systems.

📘 Introduction

Computer Systems Servicing is designed to equip learners with the knowledge and hands-on skills needed to install, configure, and maintain computer systems. This course covers both hardware and software aspects, preparing students for technical support and IT service roles.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Identify and understand computer hardware components.
  • Install and configure operating systems and application software.
  • Perform preventive maintenance and troubleshooting techniques.
  • Apply safety procedures in handling computer systems.
  • Provide technical support and customer service effectively.

🏆 Goals

By the end of this course, learners will be able to confidently service and maintain computer systems, ensuring their optimal performance. Graduates will be prepared for careers in IT support, technical servicing, and system administration, or to pursue advanced certifications in the field.
